# Agent evaluation

Compares the agent's draft notes with my own answers for 5 papers.

**How to fill in**
- **My own answer:** write it from the paper *before* reading the agent's draft.
- **Agent's answer:** copy it from the draft in `drafts/`.
- **Score:** `correct`, `partly` or `wrong`.
  - `correct`: matches the paper, nothing missing or invented.
  - `partly`: right but incomplete, vague, or has a small error.
  - `wrong`: wrong, invented, or says `not stated` when the paper does state it.
- **Notes on errors:** what went wrong, e.g. invented citation, missed limitation,
  wrong sample size, connection not supported by the cited note.

"My reaction" is left out because the agent always leaves it blank.
